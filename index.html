Claude's recommendation

Ranked by: beach proximity, modern villa vibe, pool, fits 22, within ₹3.5–4.5K budget. Patong no longer required — any beach within 30-35 min works.

1

The View PhuketTop pick — zero logistics headaches

Boutique hotel with sea views, walking distance to Kata Beach, everyone gets their own room
11 rooms for 22 ₹5,500 pp/night 5 min to Kata Beach Sea views Hotel service

The simplest, most hassle-free option for 22 people. Every pair gets their own proper hotel room with private bathroom, AC, daily housekeeping, and sea views. No splitting across 4-8 separate villas, no "who gets the good room" drama, no coordinating check-ins across multiple properties. Walk to Kata Beach in 5 minutes — Kata is arguably Phuket's best beach (cleaner and less crowded than Patong). Kata's own nightlife scene has plenty of bars, rooftop spots, and restaurants, and Patong is just a 15-min cab away. You get concierge, front desk, room service, and professional staff handling everything. For a grad trip with 22 people, the hotel format actually makes more sense than villa-sharing — everyone has privacy, proper facilities, and zero coordination overhead.

Caveat: ~₹1K over budget per person. Not a "villa vibe." No private pool per room. But the convenience premium for 22 people might be worth it.
